# Terms & Regulations: review notes

The `/terms` page and booking signature popup share `src/data/rentalTerms.js`.
This is proposed website wording, not a legal opinion or a certification of
enforceability. No deployment was performed as part of this change.

## Business policy confirmed

- Booking-payment-only reservations qualify for refunds when the applicable
  cancellation deadline is met: 48 hours before camping, or 12 hours before
  camping for reservations made less than 48 hours beforehand.
- Full payment makes rental payments non-refundable for customer cancellations,
  including advance reservations subsequently paid in full.
- The security deposit remains distinct from booking and rental payments.
- Statutory remedies are expressly preserved. A blanket non-refundable label
  does not establish that retaining all payments is fair in every circumstance.

## Drafting changes to review before publication

- Liability is tied to renter-caused loss and reasonable evidenced costs, with
  normal wear and pre-existing defects excluded. Replacement assessments take
  age and condition into account; deductions can be questioned.
- The original blanket injury exclusion is replaced with responsibility for
  safe use while preserving QashCamp's liability for negligence and legal duties.
- The confirmed return date/time governs, instead of an open-ended promise that
  equipment can be returned the next day. Actual late charges and deposit amounts
  must be disclosed in the booking process; this draft invents no rates.
- The 2–3 working-day deposit period is measured from equipment return and
  inspection. Weekends and Selangor public holidays are excluded. Confirm that
  operations can support the stated itemised assessments and timely return of
  undisputed balances.
- Cancellation deadlines use the confirmed camping start time in Malaysia time,
  and the time a cancellation reaches QashCamp. Ensure that confirmations provide
  that start time. A booking made within 12 hours cannot meet the 12-hour deadline.
- General setup order is retained, with model-specific instructions taking
  priority. Safety guidance must also match the equipment actually supplied.
- Privacy text describes required protections, not a verified audit of actual
  practices. Complete the collection-stage privacy notice with the operator's
  identity, actual data categories, sources, recipient classes, purposes,
  mandatory/optional fields, rights/contact procedure, retention arrangements and
  other applicable disclosures. Provide the required Bahasa Malaysia and English
  notices. Confirm how identity documents, payment details and signatures are
  stored and shared before making detailed privacy promises.
- Existing poster images are not rewritten. Review them for consistency before
  continuing to distribute them as contractual terms.
